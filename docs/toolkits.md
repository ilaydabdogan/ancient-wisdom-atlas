# The Atlas Toolkits — measured patterns, for working hands

Practical bridges from the Atlas's evidence to modern craft. Each toolkit
follows the same discipline: every pattern carries a **measured frequency**
from the indexes (never an assertion), at least one **verbatim quote**
verified line-by-line against a public-domain source text, a **modern name**
that connects the ancient pattern to its contemporary vocabulary, and an
honest-caveat block stating exactly what the evidence does and does not
support. Patterns that could not be verified were dropped, not padded.

They are checklists, not formulas. The oldest practitioners broke these
patterns as often as they kept them; so should you.
