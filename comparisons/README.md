# Comparisons

This directory is the readable similarity layer of the atlas.

Use it in three levels:

- [Motif Occurrence Index](motif-index.md): generated from extraction records; shows where motif tags appear.
- Curated comparison pages: human-readable cross-cultural pages with tables, evidence links, and cautions.
- Pattern essays in [patterns](../patterns): broader interpretive essays about motif families.

## Reading Rule

An occurrence means a passage has evidence for a motif. A comparison means two or more occurrences share a function, image, or narrative role. Historical contact is a stronger claim and must be marked separately.
