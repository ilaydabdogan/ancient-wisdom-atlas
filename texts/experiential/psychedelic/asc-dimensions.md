# Altered States of Consciousness — 5D-ASC Dimensions

The 5D-ASC is a self-report instrument designed to measure the structure of altered states of consciousness across five primary dimensions. The instrument was developed to find common phenomenological structure across very different altered-state inducers — psychedelic substances, sensory deprivation, hypnagogia, meditative absorption — by separating the kinds of experience that recur regardless of how the state was reached. The five dimensions below describe the territories of experience the instrument distinguishes.

## Oceanic Boundlessness — Positive Self-Loss

The experience is one of dissolution into a wider whole, accompanied by overwhelmingly positive feeling. The boundary between self and surroundings grows indistinct and then disappears. What remains is unity — with the room, with all life, with the cosmos. The emotional tone is bliss, peace, awe, gratitude, love. The state is often described as the most positively-felt experience the person has ever known. There is a sense of being at home in being itself.

## Oceanic Boundlessness — Timelessness and Vastness

Time and space lose their ordinary structure. The experience feels timeless or to contain all time at once. The sense of being located in a particular place gives way to a felt vastness — boundless, unconfined. The state is felt as larger than any earthly setting could be.

## Anxious Ego Dissolution — Threatening Self-Loss

The same dissolution of self can be felt as profoundly threatening. The boundary that ordinarily contains the self gives way, and the experiencer feels they are coming apart, being annihilated, being absorbed against their will. The emotional tone is anxiety, terror, panic. The state is sometimes felt as an attack on the integrity of the person, sometimes as a loss of control too rapid to resist.

## Anxious Ego Dissolution — Paranoia and Persecution

The experiencer feels watched, threatened, or persecuted. Ordinary trust in the surrounding environment evaporates. Other people's intentions are suspected. Inanimate features of the environment can take on a malevolent quality. The state is felt as exposure — as if hidden hostile intentions in the world have suddenly become visible.

## Anxious Ego Dissolution — Loss of Thought Control

Ordinary thought becomes uncontrollable. Thoughts loop, intrude, accelerate, shatter, repeat. The person can no longer steer attention voluntarily. The experience can feel like being caught in a runaway machine of mind that one had not realized was running unaided all along.

## Visionary Restructuralization — Elementary Imagery

Closed-eye and sometimes open-eye imagery presents itself: lattices, gratings, tunnels, spirals, cobweb structures, kaleidoscopic patterns. The imagery is described as elementary in the sense that it appears prior to figurative content — the primitive forms out of which more complex imagery is built.

## Visionary Restructuralization — Complex Imagery and Scenes

Figurative imagery presents itself: faces, beings, landscapes, scenes, narratives. The imagery is vivid, often three-dimensional, often strikingly meaningful. Sometimes it appears unbidden and proceeds with its own logic. Volunteers sometimes report scenes from places they have never been, periods they have not lived in, or entirely otherworldly content.

## Visionary Restructuralization — Audiovisual Synesthesia

Sense modalities cross. Sound generates color or shape; visual stimuli are heard; the texture of cloth carries a flavor. The crossing is felt as a feature of the experience itself rather than an interpretation. The synesthesia is sometimes pleasant and revelatory, sometimes overwhelming.

## Visionary Restructuralization — Changed Meaning of Percepts

Ordinary objects and events take on new significance. A leaf, a doorway, a stranger's gesture appears charged with meaning that ordinary perception had been filtering out. The world is felt as more eloquent, more mythic, more saturated with intention.

## Auditory Alterations — Changed Sound Perception

Sounds become more vivid, more distinct, or distorted in pitch, tempo, or texture. Music can become extraordinarily moving — felt as carrying meaning the listener had not heard in it before. Voices, ambient sounds, or silence itself can take on unfamiliar qualities.

## Auditory Alterations — Hallucinatory Sounds

Sounds present themselves that have no apparent external source — voices, music, environmental noises that are not occurring in the environment. The experiencer recognizes them as part of the altered state but they are heard as sounds rather than imagined.

## Vigilance Reduction — Reduction in Wakefulness

The state may include reductions in clarity, alertness, and orientation. Attention is harder to direct; thought is less efficient; memory of what is currently happening is patchy. The experiencer may move between dream-like and waking states without clear transitions.

## Vigilance Reduction — Drowsiness and Disorganization

Reduced vigilance can shade into drowsiness, sleepiness, dream-fragments intruding into the waking field, and a general disorganization of cognition. The state is often described as fluid — neither sleep nor ordinary waking — with the structures that ordinarily distinguish the two becoming indistinct.
